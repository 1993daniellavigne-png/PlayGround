Field notes

How to keep the hollow

Farm by day

Walk to the field and use the hoe, seeds, and watering can. Crops grow at dawn only if watered.

  • 1–5 or tap a slot to choose
  • Space or Act uses it on the nearest plot
  • Tap or click a plot to walk over and tend it

Raise lantern wards

Tap or click a carved stone pad to build. Lanterns strike, sprinklers slow, bramble totems burst.

  • Tap or click a built ward to upgrade or sell
  • Wards fire by themselves, day or night
  • Rest at the farmhouse door to call dusk

Defend by night

Shadows follow the old path to your hearth. If the hearth falls, the farm is lost.

  • F or tap the ground to cast a lantern flare
  • Keep the path covered with wards
  • Survive seven nights to win
Refill at the well or pond. Harvest and clear nights earn moon coins.
